# 🌿 PSYPLATFORM

## *A modern platform connecting psychologists and patients safely and intuitively.*

# 🚀 OVERVIEW

PsyPlatform is a web application designed to enhance the therapeutic
relationship by providing intuitive tools for session management,
progress tracking, and secure communication. The platform prioritizes
privacy, security, and usability, ensuring a smooth experience for both
professionals and patients. It is available in two languages: Italian
and English.

# ✨ KEY FEATURES

## 👩‍⚕️ For Psychologists

-   Smart Dashboard --- everything in one actionable control panel.
-   Advanced Progress Tracking --- dynamic charts and custom metrics.
-   Time Management Tools --- calendar, scheduling, and reminders.
-   Ultra-Secure Environment --- end-to-end encryption & GDPR compliant.

## 👤 For Patients

-   Private Personal Diary --- a secure place for thoughts &
    reflections.
-   Visual Progress Tracking --- clear charts for personal growth.
-   Session Preparation Tools --- reminders and streamlined notes.
-   Maximum Privacy --- Italian-based servers with advanced protection.

# 🛠️ TECH STACK
