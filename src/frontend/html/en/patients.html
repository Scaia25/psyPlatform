FEATURES FOR PATIENTS

📱 Personal Diary

Your private and secure space to collect thoughts, emotions and reflections between sessions. Track your daily mood trends and share what you consider important with your psychologist in an organized and structured way. A simple but powerful tool to improve the effectiveness of your therapeutic journey.

📈 See Your Progress

View the progress of your journey through clear and intuitive charts that track your mood and progress over time. Observe the evolution of your well-being session after session, with indicators that help you recognize improvements. A concrete and motivating way to see the results of your inner work and maintain awareness of the path you've taken.

🔔 Prepare at Your Best

Forget manual management of appointments and reminders. Our platform automates scheduling and sends automatic reminders to you and your psychologist. Organize notes from each session in a structured way and access your therapy history with one click, ensuring you're always prepared and focused on your healing journey.

🔐 Total Privacy

Your data is protected with end-to-end encryption, ensuring that only you and your psychologist can access the information. The platform is fully GDPR compliant and respects the rigorous ethical standards of the psychological profession. Servers located in Italy and automatic backups ensure maximum security and service continuity for your peace of mind.
