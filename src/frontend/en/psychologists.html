FEATURES FOR PSYCHOLOGISTS

🎯 Centralized Dashboard

A unified space to manage your entire therapeutic practice. View in real-time the progress of all patients, access session notes and monitor key parameters like mood and progress towards goals. All organizational tools - from calendar to appointments - integrated into one intuitive interface. Your complete overview, always updated and protected.

📊 Progress Tracker

Monitor therapeutic progress in real-time with our intuitive charts and customizable metrics. Each patient has a clear view of their journey, while you can identify important trends and patterns based on objective data, thus enriching your clinical work with immediate insights.

⏰ Save Time

Forget manual management of appointments and reminders. Our platform automates scheduling and sends automatic reminders to you and your patients. Organize notes from each session in a structured way and access clinical history with one click, freeing up precious hours to dedicate to therapy.

🔒 Secure and Compliant

Your patients' data is protected with end-to-end encryption, ensuring that only you and the patient can access the information. The platform is fully GDPR compliant and respects the rigorous ethical standards of the psychological profession. Servers located in Italy and automatic backups ensure maximum security and service continuity.
