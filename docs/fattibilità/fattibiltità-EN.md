# Feasibility Study – PsyPlatform  
v1.0.0 – 17/11/2025 – Giorgio Cominelli, Simone Scainelli

---

# Executive Summary

### **Project Description**  
PsyPlatform is a digital platform designed to facilitate communication and the therapeutic workflow between psychologists and patients, providing secure and intuitive tools compliant with privacy regulations.

### **Purpose of the Study**  
Evaluate the technical, economic, organizational, and market feasibility of developing the platform.

### **Final Recommendation**  
**FEASIBLE WITH CONDITIONS** – The project is viable, but requires advanced competencies in security, handling sensitive data, and stable infrastructure.

### **Estimated Investment**  
€ 8,000 – € 20,000

### **Expected ROI**  
30–60% within 2–3 years.

### **Main Risks**
- GDPR compliance and handling sensitive data  
- Competitors already established in the market  
- High requirements for security and service reliability  
- Technical complexity of integrated video calls  
- Slow adoption rate among professionals

### **Business Activity**  
Digital service based on a SaaS subscription model.

# 2. Project Description

## 2.1 Project Objectives

### **Main Objectives**
- Enable secure communication between psychologists and patients  
- Manage appointments, sessions, and reminders  
- Store clinical notes  
- Ensure high data security  

### **Secondary Objectives**
- Integrate secure video calls  
- Provide progress analytics  
- Develop a mobile app  

### **Success KPIs**
- Number of psychologists subscribed  
- Monthly booking rate  
- 90-day user retention  
- Service reliability (SLA > 99%)

---

## 2.2 Main Features

### **Key Functionalities**
- Appointment scheduling  
- Secure messaging  
- Encrypted video calls  
- Clinical notes and patient history  
- Reminders and notifications  
- Online exercises and questionnaires  
- Dashboard for therapists

---

## 2.3 Core Requirements

### **Technical**
- Backend: Java/Spring or Python/Django  
- Encrypted database (PostgreSQL or MongoDB)  
- Cloud hosting (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure)  
- End-to-end encryption  

### **Operational**
- User support  
- 24/7 monitoring  
- Regular updates  

### **Regulatory**
- GDPR  
- Health data management  
- Dual consent  
- Encryption at rest and in transit
