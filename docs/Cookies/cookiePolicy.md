# Cookie Policy – PsyPlatform

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how PsyPlatform uses them, and the choices users have regarding their use.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on a user’s device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when visiting a website.  
They are widely used to make websites work properly, improve user experience, and provide information to website owners.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies Used by PsyPlatform

PsyPlatform uses different types of cookies for specific purposes.

### 2.1 Technical Cookies (Strictly Necessary)
These cookies are essential for the correct functioning of the platform and cannot be disabled.

They are used to:
- Manage user authentication and login sessions  
- Maintain security during navigation  
- Remember user preferences (such as language)  
- Ensure proper functioning of protected areas  

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest and service provision.

---

### 2.2 Functional Cookies
These cookies improve usability and personalization of the platform.

They allow:
- Saving user preferences  
- Customizing the interface  
- Improving navigation experience  

These cookies do not collect personal data for marketing purposes.

---

### 2.3 Analytical Cookies (Anonymous)
PsyPlatform may use analytical cookies in an **anonymous and aggregated form** to understand how users interact with the platform.

They are used to:
- Analyze platform usage  
- Improve features and performance  
- Identify technical issues  

No personally identifiable information is collected.

---

### 2.4 Third-Party Cookies
At the moment, PsyPlatform **does not use third-party profiling or advertising cookies**.  
If future integrations are added, users will be informed and asked for explicit consent.

---

## 3. Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are used to:
- Guarantee platform security  
- Improve user experience  
- Optimize performance  
- Ensure correct functionality of services  

PsyPlatform **does not use cookies for advertising or profiling purposes**.

---

## 4. Cookie Management

Users can manage or disable cookies through their browser settings.  
Disabling technical cookies may affect the correct functioning of the platform.

Instructions for managing cookies can be found in the help section of the user’s browser.

---

## 5. Data Protection and Privacy

All data collected through cookies is processed in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the **GDPR**.  
Sensitive information related to psychological activity is **never collected through cookies**.

For more information, please refer to the Privacy Policy.

---

## 6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated to reflect changes in legal requirements or platform features.  
Users will be notified of significant changes.

---

For questions regarding this Cookie Policy, users can contact the PsyPlatform support team.
